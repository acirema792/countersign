    The one rule that matters

    An amount or payee that appears only in untrusted email text can never move money on its own. It must match a figure the business already holds: an order line, an open bill, a vendor's PayPal address on file, a catalog price. Otherwise a human countersigns.

    What the model is not allowed to decide

    Whether money moves, how much, or to whom: PayPal only receives the payload Countersign rebuilds from the books, never the model's raw arguments. The model never sees the policy code, never holds PayPal credentials, and has no tool that approves its own actions. Approvals go through a separate owner endpoint that needs the owner PIN.

    Hash-chained receipts

    Every decision and every execution is appended to a SHA-256 chain. The head is signed with Ed25519 (public key at /api/audit/pubkey; full export at /api/audit/export). Re-verify any time at /api/audit/verify. The log lives in memory and starts again when the free server restarts. It is shared by every visitor of this demo; payees from "Write your own" emails are not recorded in it.
